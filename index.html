Our Story & Commitment

Shri Jagdamba Industry — Committed to Pure Health in Every Drop

Rooted in Uruli Dewachi, Pune, Nuova was founded with a singular conviction: We're not just selling oil, we're saving lives. Modern adulteration and chemical solvent refining have compromised kitchen health.

By choosing only A1 Grade sun-dried seeds and extracting under gentle, unheated pressure, Nuova brings back the authentic, nutrient-dense oils our ancestors relied upon for longevity and vitality.
